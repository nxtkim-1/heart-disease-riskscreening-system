Why threshold 0.39

In screening, missing an at-risk patient carries far more human cost than ordering one extra test. Lowering the cutoff from 0.50 to 0.39 halved missed cases from 16 to 8 on the 184-patient cohort — paired here with 95% uncertainty intervals and honest hospital-shift limits.
